# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while discussing ideas, and criticize the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or sexualized attention.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
